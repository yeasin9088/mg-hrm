# Operator notification policy decisions — 23 October 2026

The owner selected these options during implementation. They apply to the
existing Notification Center and To-Do/Follow-Up path, not a standalone screen.

| Setting | Selected pilot policy | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine Push window | 09:00–18:00 Asia/Dhaka | A real deadline retains its actual timestamp. Communication and recording manager decisions remain available 24 hours. This Push window does not define holidays or authorize office execution outside 09:00–18:00. |
| Digest times | 09:05 and 17:15 Asia/Dhaka | Aggregate eligible backlog; do not send one Push per overnight item. A verified workday calendar is not yet available. |
| Rolling per-item cap | P3 routine: 1; P2 important: 2 per 24 hours | P1 real deadline risk needs separate tested, bounded handling; P0 requires a verified emergency process and explicit opt-in. Read does not replenish a quota. |

These selections approve the specified pilot values, not an invented payroll
cutoff, holiday calendar, emergency procedure, external approval or automatic
HR action. Activation remains contingent on integrated source, delivery, mobile
and rollback tests.
